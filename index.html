Why Serpa exists

Small teams lose days to tool sprawl.

An SEO practitioner juggles separate logins, separate dashboards and scripts that only run on one laptop. Serpa puts them behind a single entry point.

Fewer logins

Every product signs in with the same Google account and stays signed in.

Operator control

Each product exposes its admin panel and its technical internals from the same place.

Native app

Packaged as a Mac application, built and released the same way as every other tool in the family.

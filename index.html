Why Serpa exists

Small teams lose days to tool sprawl.

An SEO practitioner juggles separate logins, separate dashboards and scripts that only run on one laptop. Serpa puts them behind a single entry point.

Flagship product

Indexa

A marketplace where publishers list sponsored articles and backlinks, and advertisers buy them with clear pricing and clear placement rules.

Built for the Algerian market, where this kind of buying is mostly informal and handled through private messages.

PublishersAdvertisersAlgeria first
How a purchase flows
  1. ListA publisher describes a site, its topics and its price per article or link.
  2. OrderAn advertiser picks a placement and submits the brief.
  3. PublishThe publisher posts the article with the agreed link.
  4. VerifyThe link and its attributes are checked before the order closes.
